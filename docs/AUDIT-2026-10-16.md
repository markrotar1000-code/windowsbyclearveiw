- **Do not “correct” the email domain.** Browser audit called `owner@windowsbyclearveiw.com` a typo. Repository policy explicitly keeps production mail on that legacy domain until a canonical-domain mailbox exists and is tested. The different public web spelling is intentional.
- **Four versus six signs is not necessarily inconsistent.** The guide describes six signs, while its new graphic specifically depicts four. Its alt text accurately enumerates four. Do not rewrite the graphic or guide count as though they were the same list.
- **Woodland already has its own county label.** Current city data and the area-list item say Cowlitz County. Broader Clark County positioning includes nearby Woodland; the browser report's claim that every individual town is labelled Clark County is not supported by current source. No county facts changed.
- Public-profile name spacing and different inboxes merit owner review, but do not establish a broken contact path. No directory profile was modified.
- A shell-based crawl received environment-dependent 403 responses while the browser audit reached the public pages; those are not evidence the site is down.
